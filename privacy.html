Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026.

Threads Research is a read-only research tool that helps users search and review public Threads posts through Meta's official Threads API.

The service processes only the information needed to connect the user's Threads account, perform a requested search, and display public Threads results.

The review demonstration does not retain public search results after the session ends. The project does not provide publishing, replying, deletion, moderation, messaging, or account-management features.

Threads Research does not sell user information and does not use Threads data for advertising.

For deletion information, see Data Deletion Instructions.

If the production deployment model changes, this policy will be updated before launch.
