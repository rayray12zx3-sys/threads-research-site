Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026.

Threads Research is a read-only research tool that helps users search and review public Threads posts through Meta's official Threads API.

The service processes only the information needed to connect the user's Threads account, perform a requested search, and display public Threads results.

The hosted reviewer demonstration uses Railway Corporation for server hosting in the selected US East (Virginia, USA) region. Its server processes OAuth credentials and each consenting user’s token, profile, own posts, and requested public search results. App secrets remain in server-side environment variables; access tokens remain in isolated server memory for at most 30 minutes and can be cleared by the user. Profile and post bodies are not persisted by the application. Railway may retain operational/network logs under its own Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum. The selected hosting region does not establish every possible remote-support access country; that complete provider disclosure is still being verified before App Review submission.

The review demonstration does not retain public search results after the session ends. The project does not provide publishing, replying, deletion, moderation, messaging, or account-management features.

Threads Research does not sell user information and does not use Threads data for advertising.

For deletion information, see Data Deletion Instructions.

If the production deployment model changes, this policy will be updated before launch.
