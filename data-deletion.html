Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated October 4, 2026.

The App Review demonstration keeps account authorization only for the active session. Ending the session or using the clear-session action removes it from the demonstration environment.

Users may also revoke the application's Threads authorization from their Meta account settings.

If you believe Threads Research has retained information associated with your use of the service, use the contact method listed in the public repository or Meta application listing to request deletion. Do not send passwords or other confidential credentials.

If a future production deployment changes the storage model, these instructions will be updated before launch.
